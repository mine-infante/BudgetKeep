# Objective

Formally close Phase 02 (Business Analysis) and establish the official business baseline that will support the Architecture, UX, Development and Quality Assurance phases.

---

# Phase Summary

Phase 02 transformed the approved Product Vision into a complete Business Analysis Specification describing the business behavior expected for BudgetKeep.

The analysis defined the official business domain, business processes, business rules, functional capabilities, functional requirements, non-functional requirements, assumptions, constraints and traceability model.

During this phase, several strategic concepts were clarified and incorporated into the Business Domain Model without changing the product vision or the MVP scope.

The Project Office reviewed the resulting documentation and approved the business baseline.

---

# Deliverables

The following documents were completed and approved during Phase 02:

- Business_Analysis_Specification_v1.0.md
- Business_Domain_Specification_v1.0.md
- Artifact_Naming_Standard.md (updated)

---

# Key Decisions

The following business decisions were consolidated during this phase:

- Financial Availability represents the financial resources selected by the User for analysis.
- Financial Resource became an official Domain Concept.
- Financial Strategy represents recommendations generated by BudgetKeep.
- Financial Plan represents the decisions adopted by the User.
- Exchange Rate became an independent Domain Concept supporting the multi-currency business model.
- Functional Capabilities became the official organizational layer between Business Processes and Functional Requirements.

---

# Methodology Improvements

During this phase, AI MineSoftware evolved with the incorporation of the following practices:

- Functional Capability Model (FCM) as an official Business Analysis artifact.
- Separation of the Business Domain into an independent Business Domain Specification.
- Single source of truth for Domain Concepts.
- Improved traceability between Business Processes, Functional Capabilities and Functional Requirements.

---

# Lessons Learned

Separating large business artifacts into specialized specifications significantly improves maintainability, traceability and governance without affecting the consistency of the Business Analysis.

The incorporation of Functional Capabilities also proved to be an effective organizational layer between business processes and functional requirements, improving communication with Architecture, UX, Development and QA.
