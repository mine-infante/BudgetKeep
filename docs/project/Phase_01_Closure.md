# Objective

Formally close Phase 01 (Product Discovery) and establish the official baseline for the following phases of the BudgetKeep project.

---

# Phase Summary

Phase 01 focused on understanding the product before defining requirements, architecture or implementation.

The goal was to establish a clear and shared understanding of:

- The product identity.
- The business problem.
- The target users.
- The product philosophy.
- The long-term vision.
- The MVP scope.
- The strategic principles that will guide all future decisions.

The Product Vision was reviewed and approved by the Project Office and became the official foundation of the project.

---

# Deliverables

The following documents were completed and approved during Phase 01:

- Product_Vision_v1.0.md
- Decision_Log.md
- Git_Manual.md

---

# Key Decisions

The following strategic decisions were established during this phase:

- BudgetKeep is not a money management system.
- BudgetKeep exists to reduce financial uncertainty.
- The Financial Reality Model becomes the central business concept.
- Artificial Intelligence recommends; the user always decides.
- The MVP focuses on payments and due dates.
- Every future feature must contribute to reducing financial uncertainty.

---

# Methodology Improvements

During this phase, AI MineSoftware evolved with the incorporation of the following practices:

- Project Office review before approving deliverables.
- Official document versioning.
- Decision Log as a permanent governance artifact.
- Documentation-first approach before software implementation.
- Git used not only for source code but also for project knowledge.

---

# Exit Criteria

Phase 01 is considered complete because:

- The Product Vision has been approved.
- Strategic decisions have been documented.
- Official documentation has been versioned.
- Documentation has been committed and published to GitHub.

# Lessons Learned

Several improvements to the original methodology were identified during this phase.

The most important was recognizing that prompts generate working drafts, while the Project Office is responsible for reviewing, approving and publishing official project documentation.

This governance process will be applied to all subsequent phases.
